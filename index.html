Your Alameda Kitchen Experts

We Know Alameda Homes

Alameda Island is a community of architectural treasures -- from the grand Victorians along San Antonio Avenue to the cozy Craftsman bungalows of the East End and the modern builds in Bay Farm. Each style brings its own kitchen challenges and opportunities.

With 21+ years of experience working specifically in Alameda, we understand the permitting process, the structural nuances of older homes, and how to design kitchens that honor the island's character while delivering every modern convenience.

Whether you're updating a galley kitchen in a 1905 Victorian or building an open-concept chef's kitchen in a newer home, we have the expertise to make it happen. Financing is available to help bring your vision to life.
